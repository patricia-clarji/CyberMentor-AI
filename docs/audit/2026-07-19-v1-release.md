## Trust boundary

`v1-release-baseline` is an explicit, narrow release approval for the initial academy, attributed to the product owner without inventing reviewer identities. It cannot authorize later changes. Normal publications still require the exact technical, instructional, accessibility, licensing, and conditional safety reviews plus an authorized publisher. AI-assisted drafts remain labeled and non-publishable until that workflow completes.

## Release disposition

The local-first Version 1 academy is immediately usable after installation and no longer has an empty-content blocker. It is not yet a production multi-user SaaS: authentication, tenant-safe persistence, hosted role workflows, real browser E2E coverage, and hardened lab infrastructure remain production blockers for that deployment model.
