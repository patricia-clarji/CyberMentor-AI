# Company training portal

The `/company` experience supports employee cohorts, assigned training, completion monitoring, protected reports, and organization-scoped evidence.

Company managers primarily receive aggregate metrics. Individual learner evidence requires the detailed-progress permission and is restricted to active organization members. Personal learning from another organization cannot appear because enrolments, assignments, skill state, and completions are tenant-filtered.

Supported role-profile content IDs may reference junior SOC analyst, help-desk awareness, IT administrator foundations, secure-coding foundations, or general employee awareness when that versioned content exists. Assignments never silently change their linked content version after creation.

Readiness is presented only as evidence-derived dimensions or completion rates with definitions, source, timestamp, and limitations. It is not an employment, compliance, or role-readiness guarantee.
