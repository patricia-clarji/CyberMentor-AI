# University and training-provider portal

The `/university` experience reuses programmes, cohorts, assignments, reviews, learning evidence, projects, labs, missions, and completion records.

Programmes record an organization-stable key, academic period, optional internal qualification label, required and elective pathways, required projects, completion policy, staff IDs, status, and version. No accreditation is inferred from the qualification label.

Cohorts support academic course, bootcamp, employee training, certification preparation, and private group types. They record dates, status, programme, completion expectations, visibility rules, self-enrolment policy, creator, timestamps, and version. Staff, learners, and version-pinned curriculum are normalized records.

Reports and dashboards distinguish platform event completion from human-reviewed evidence. Estimated mastery remains labelled with confidence and is never represented as accredited completion.
